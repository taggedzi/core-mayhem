# Code of Conduct

Core Mayhem welcomes players and contributors of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Everyone participating in this project is expected to help keep its
community respectful, safe, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, disability,
  experience, or level of participation.
- Critique ideas, code, and gameplay decisions without attacking people.
- Give useful feedback, accept constructive criticism, and respect boundaries.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, listen when someone describes harm, and
  take responsibility for correcting mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for different perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, sexualized language, or sexual imagery in community
  spaces.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or helping with a report.
- Continuing unwanted contact or behavior after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews,
and other community spaces managed by the project. It also applies when someone
represents Core Mayhem publicly in an official capacity.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns to a repository maintainer using a private contact method listed
on their GitHub profile, if one is available. Include relevant links, dates, and
enough context to explain what happened. Share only information needed to review
the concern.

The project does not currently publish a dedicated private reporting address.
If you cannot find a private maintainer contact, use GitHub's **Report content**
or **Report abuse** controls for conduct on GitHub. Avoid posting sensitive
reports or personal information in public issues. You do not need to confront
the person involved before reporting.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share identifying details only
when needed to investigate or respond. Complete confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. A maintainer involved in a report should leave its review to another
maintainer when one is available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, or restrict
participation to protect the community. Responses should reflect the severity,
impact, and pattern of behavior. They may include a request to change behavior,
a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may
warrant immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

When practical, maintainers should explain the decision and any conditions for
returning. Someone affected by a decision may request reconsideration through
the same private contact method, providing relevant context. Another maintainer
should review that request when available.
