## Original synthesized effects: MIT

All active game sound effects are synthesized from oscillators, deterministic
noise, envelopes, filters, and echoes. No third-party recordings, samples,
or reference audio are used by the generators. The generator code and
resulting audio files are covered by the root MIT license.

The 14 combat files under `core-mayhem/src/audio/generated/` are:

- `activate-buff.wav`
- `activate-debuff.wav`
- `core-death.wav`
- `core-repair.wav`
- `core-shield-down.wav`
- `core-shield-up.wav`
- `fire-cannon.wav`
- `fire-laser.wav`
- `fire-missile.wav`
- `fire-mortar.wav`
- `impact-armor.wav`
- `impact-core.wav`
- `impact-miss.wav`
- `impact-shield.wav`

Regenerate them with `npm run audio:generate` from `core-mayhem/`.
The source is `core-mayhem/scripts/generate-sfx.mjs`. Files are mono,
44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, with fixed seeds for reproducible output.

The separate `core-mayhem/src/audio/generated-low-health-alarm.wav`
is generated by `core-mayhem/scripts/generate-alarm.mjs` and is also MIT
licensed. It uses mono, 22.05 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV.

These effects were developed with Codex assistance. Preserve the MIT license
when redistributing them. They are not adaptations of the archived recordings.

## Excluded archive: not part of releases

The root `unused/` directory is ignored by Git and lies outside the Vite
application's public directory. Its contents must not be included in releases.

- `unused/helton-yan-sfx/`: the previous 14 combat recordings and their
  local notice. Pixel Combat Sound Pack by Helton Yan (source page also
  credits Beto Bezerra), https://heltonyan.itch.io/pixelcombat , under
  CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Project filenames
  were renamed and converted from WAV to Ogg Vorbis; further editing history
  is undocumented. These files are no longer loaded or distributed by the
  game. Any separate redistribution remains subject to CC BY 4.0, including
  attribution and change notices.
- `unused/cyborg/`: unused graphics, manifest, and audio; ownership and reuse
  terms have not been established.
- `unused/announcer/`: John Carroll's Announcer Voice Pack recordings.
  Source: https://johncarroll.itch.io/announcer-voice-pack . The published page
  uses custom terms; it does not state CC BY 4.0. Redistribution permission
  has not been established. Recorded announcements are disabled.
- `unused/audio/core_alarm.ogg`: previous alarm, with conflicting attribution.
  The old notice named Robinhood76 but linked to
  https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise , which credits aquinn
  and lists CC0. The recording's actual source could not be verified, so it
  was excluded rather than assigning it an unverified license.

The archive is retained locally for reference. Neither MIT nor this document
grants additional permission to redistribute these archived files. The
Helton Yan recordings retain their existing CC BY 4.0 license.

## Distribution

Vite copies `core-mayhem/public/` into the production site. The build includes
copies of this notice, the code license, and third-party software notices in
`licenses/`. The game's Credits link makes those notices accessible to players.
Keep these notices with redistributed builds. Generated effects and their
source code are tracked in Git. Music remains excluded from Git; downstream
users need separate permission for any music they redistribute.
